# Support

For help with a MagisterialModels project:

1. Read the project's README, documentation, and existing issues.
2. Use that project's Discussions area if enabled for questions and general guidance.
3. Open a focused issue for reproducible bugs or specific improvement requests.

When asking for help, describe your goal, what you tried, what happened, and your environment. Redact credentials, private URLs, tokens, and personal information before sharing logs.

For suspected security vulnerabilities, follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) and avoid public disclosure.

Community support is best-effort; response times and feature availability differ across repositories.
