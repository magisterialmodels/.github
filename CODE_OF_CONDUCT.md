# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want MagisterialModels spaces to be welcoming, constructive, and respectful to everyone who participates.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect and assume good faith.
- Offer specific, actionable feedback and accept criticism professionally.
- Stay focused on the work, evidence, and ideas under discussion.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing others' personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, hate speech, targeted insults, sexual harassment, intimidation, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

To report a conduct concern, contact organization maintainers privately through an appropriate available channel. Please do not publish sensitive reports in issues or discussions. If no private channel is available, do not include sensitive details in a public post; request a private contact method instead.

Maintainers will consider reports in context and take proportionate action.
